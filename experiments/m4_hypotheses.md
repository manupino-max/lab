# M4 hypotheses

H_M4_1: There exists alpha<1 on the midpoint mean-line intervention that improves protected-group fairness while preserving task utility.

H_M4_2: The midpoint m=(mu0+mu1)/2 is a useful common fairness mean.

H_M4_3: Treating groups as diagonal Gaussians, moving means along their connecting line reduces distributional separation; equal means alone are insufficient if covariance differs.

Falsification: no alpha<1 meets the frozen utility tolerance while improving fairness, or midpoint is dominated by all tested alternatives.
