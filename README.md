# TRIAD Lab

Public laboratory for reproducible machine-learning research experiments.

## Purpose

This repository is an **exploratory lab**. It is intended for:

- synthetic datasets and controlled benchmarks;
- experiment runners and reproducibility tooling;
- tests and continuous integration;
- exploratory and negative results;
- reusable utilities for bias, fairness, representation, and geometry experiments.

Research claims, frozen protocols, thesis evidence, and unpublished paper results are kept in the private research repositories until explicitly promoted.

## Status labels

- `TOOLING` — reusable infrastructure
- `SYNTHETIC_DEMO` — controlled synthetic experiment
- `EXPLORATORY` — investigation in progress
- `REPLICATION` — reproduction/independent check
- `PROMISING` — candidate for further validation
- `PROMOTE` — ready to transfer to the private research record

## Principle

> Explore openly; validate rigorously; promote selectively.
